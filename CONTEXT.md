# French Writing Prompter

A tool that generates AI-powered French writing prompts, evaluates submitted writing against the CEFR scale, and guides learners toward higher proficiency over time.

## Language

**Writing Prompt**:
A paragraph-long scenario with structured meta (genre, tense, tone, **Registre** `formel` / `familier` / `mixt`, topic, topic tags) generated by AI, adapted to the learner's current CEFR level. The prompt text states the registre explicitly; Student Writing should match it, and Evaluations judge Register & Style against that registre. The server attaches a word-count target from CEFR level (soft nudge only).
_Avoid_: topic as the main label for the whole prompt, question, exercise

**Student Writing**:
The text a learner submits in response to a Writing Prompt.
_Avoid_: answer, response, submission, essay

**Draft**:
Unsubmitted Student Writing saved against an active session.
_Avoid_: autosave blob, scratch text, temporary note

**Evaluation**:
An AI-generated assessment of a Student Writing across four dimensions (grammatical accuracy, vocabulary range & control, coherence & cohesion, register & style), yielding per-dimension scores, an overall CEFR level, and **Corrections** (tutor notes on quoted spans).
_Avoid_: grading, marking, feedback (Evaluation includes the scores and Corrections, not only free text)

**Correction**:
One tutor note tied to a quoted span in Student Writing. Its kind is **Mistake**, **Standard→Louisiana suggestion**, or **Dialect check**.
_Avoid_: error, fix (too vague)

**Mistake**:
Wrong in both Standard and Louisiana French; feeds **Weakness** memory.
_Avoid_: typo only when it is also ungrammatical in the learner's dialect

**Standard→Louisiana suggestion**:
Standard French that is acceptable but could be phrased in Louisiana French; never counted as a Mistake or toward quotas.
_Avoid_: error, correction (use Correction kind)

**Dialect check**:
The tutor asks whether a non-standard form is intentional Louisiana usage; **Yes** creates a **Dialect Note**, **No** makes it a Mistake.
_Avoid_: guess, assumption

**Dialect Note**:
A learner-confirmed Louisiana form the tutor never flags again.
_Avoid_: whitelist entry, custom dictionary row

**Weakness**:
A Mistake category with a trend over recent sessions.
_Avoid_: mistake list, error type

**Focus du jour**:
Up to two Weaknesses steering today's Writing Prompt constraints.
_Avoid_: theme of the day, random topic

**Resource**:
A catalog link per Weakness category; never invented by the model.
_Avoid_: suggested reading from the LLM

**Carnet**:
The page holding Weaknesses and Dialect Notes.
_Avoid_: notebook page, profile settings

**CEFR Level**:
A proficiency tier on the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages scale: A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2. Each learner has a current CEFR Level, initially self-declared or determined by a Placement Test.
_Avoid_: level, rank, grade

**Placement Test**:
A sequence of three Writing Prompts with Evaluations, used to establish a learner's initial CEFR Level when they don't self-declare.
_Avoid_: onboarding quiz, diagnostic

**Grammatical Accuracy**:
An Evaluation dimension measuring conjugations, agreements, spelling, and sentence structure.
_Avoid_: grammar (too broad)

**Vocabulary Range & Control**:
An Evaluation dimension measuring word choice, precision, and idiomatic usage.
_Avoid_: vocab, lexicon

**Coherence & Cohesion**:
An Evaluation dimension measuring logical flow, paragraph structure, and use of connectors.
_Avoid_: flow, organization

**Register & Style**:
An Evaluation dimension measuring formality, tone, rhetorical devices, and naturalness of expression.
_Avoid_: style (not specific enough), voice, tone

**French Dialect**:
The variety of French the learner practices. Currently only Louisiana French is supported. **Standard French in Student Writing is never wrong**: it yields a Standard→Louisiana suggestion and never counts as a Mistake.
_Avoid_: variant, regionalism, “not Standard French” as the default judgment

**Louisiana French** (Cajun French):
A dialect of French spoken in Louisiana. The canonical grammar and vocabulary rules live in `src/modules/dialect/louisiana-rules.ts` (single source of truth for prompts and Evaluations).
_Avoid_: dialectal French, Creole (it's a dialect of French, not a creole); inline feature lists in this file

## Example dialogue

**Dev:** When a user first arrives, how do we know their CEFR Level?
**Domain expert:** They can either self-declare (pick A1-C2) or take a Placement Test. If they self-declare, the first Evaluation may nudge them up or down.

**Dev:** And what triggers moving up a level?
**Domain expert:** Three consecutive Evaluations all scoring above the current CEFR Level. The learner can also self-promote manually if they disagree with the automated progression.

**Dev:** What do the suggestions look like?
**Domain expert:** Per-dimension specifics plus an overall holistic recommendation, both framed around what would push the learner to the next CEFR Level (e.g. "To reach B2, work on...").

**Dev:** Can a user generate unlimited Writing Prompts?
**Domain expert:** Maximum three per day, to balance practice frequency with quality reflection time.

**Dev:** What happens to a Student Writing after it's evaluated?
**Domain expert:** It's saved alongside its Evaluation and the Writing Prompt that inspired it, so the learner can review their history. One prompt = one Evaluation; you can't re-submit to the same prompt.

**Dev:** What does the AI return for an Evaluation?
**Domain expert:** A structured JSON blob: per-dimension scores, overall CEFR Level, a French summary and next-level tip, and **Corrections** (Mistake, Standard→Louisiana suggestion, or Dialect check) tied to quoted spans. Standard French never lowers a score or the overall CEFR Level.

**Dev:** How does the Placement Test work?
**Domain expert:** Three prompts back-to-back in one session. At the end, the assigned CEFR Level is shown alongside the full Evaluations for each of the three writings.

**Dev:** What if the AI call fails?
**Domain expert:** Show an error and let the learner retry or regenerate the prompt.

**Dev:** When does the 3-per-day quota reset?
**Domain expert:** Midnight local time.
